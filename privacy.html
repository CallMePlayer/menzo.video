Privacy Policy

Menzo Video · Effective date: November 10, 2026

In short: Menzo Video does not collect, store on our servers, sell or share your personal information. The app runs entirely on your device. Your audio files never leave it.

This policy explains how the Menzo Video app and website at menzo.video (the “App”) handle information. The App is provided by CallMePlayer (“we”, “us”).

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. We do not use cookies or advertising identifiers, and we do not collect your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data, or device identifiers.

2. Audio files you load

When you choose an audio file, it is read and played by your browser or device only. It is not uploaded, copied to a server or shared with anyone. The App does not keep the audio file after you close it, so you choose it again each session.

3. Information stored on your device

To work properly, the App stores a small amount of data locally on your device. This data stays on your device and is never sent to us.

WhatWhy
Scene markers you create (names and positions), saved against the name of the audio file they belong toSo your markers are still there next time you open the same audio file
A copy of the App’s own files (a “cache”)So the App opens quickly and works offline
A single flag recording that the “ready to use offline” message has been shownSo the message is only shown once

Because markers are saved against the audio file’s name, that name is kept on your device along with them. You can delete all of this at any time by clearing the site data for the App in your browser or device settings, or by uninstalling the App.

4. Hosting and server logs

The App is delivered as a web app from menzo.video. Like any website, the hosting provider’s servers may automatically record technical information when the App’s files are downloaded, such as your IP address, browser type and the time of the request. We do not use this information to identify you, and we do not combine it with any other data. Once the App has been installed, it runs offline and does not need to contact the server to work.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent, trade or share personal information with anyone, and the App does not include any third-party analytics, advertising or tracking services.

6. Permissions

The App does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts or any other sensitive permission. It only reads the audio file that you choose.

7. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because the App collects no personal information, there is nothing to remove if a child uses it.

8. Data security

The App is delivered over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Because your data stays on your own device, it is protected by your device’s own security. We recommend keeping your device and browser up to date.

9. Your rights and choices

Since we do not collect or hold personal information about you, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, export or delete. If you are in a region with data protection laws (for example the EU, UK or California) and have a question about your rights, contact us using the details below. Locally stored data can be removed at any time as described in section 3.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available at menzo.video/privacy, and the effective date at the top will change when we update it. If the App ever begins collecting information, we will update this policy first and, where required, ask for your consent.
